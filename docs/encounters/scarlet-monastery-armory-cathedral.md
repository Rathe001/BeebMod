## Notes and disagreements

- Each wing names its instance `scarletmonastery`, as the Graveyard and Library do.
- Whitemane's half-health sleep and raise, her mind control and the kill-Whitemane-first tactic are K's. The rest is W's.
- Mograine and Whitemane are one entry. The game may send their fights apart; either name shows it.
