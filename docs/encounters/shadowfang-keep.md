# Shadowfang Keep

- **Key:** `shadowfangkeep` · 8 bosses
- **Sources:** W, M, K (the Classic pages only, since several pages describe Cataclysm's keep). Npc ids V.
- **Order:** Rethilgore → Razorclaw the Butcher → Baron Silverlaine → Commander Springvale → Odo the Blindwatcher → Fenrus the Devourer → Wolf Master Nandos → Archmage Arugal
- **Left out:** Deathsworn Captain (rare).

## Disagreements

- **Veil of Shadow.** W and M say it cuts healing by 75%. K's Classic text says 50% for 8 seconds. The two Forever guides' 75% is used, with no duration.
