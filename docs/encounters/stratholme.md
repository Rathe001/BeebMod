# Stratholme (Living and Undead) · WoW: Forever

- **Key in Data.lua:** `stratholmeliving`, `stratholmeundead`
- **Sources:**
  - **W**: Warcraft Tavern, WoW Forever guide (warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/stratholme, stratholme-living and stratholme-undead), read 2026-11-07
  - **K**: the Warcraft Wiki (warcraft.wiki.gg) boss pages, read 2026-11-07. Dathrohan and Balnazzar's page was read. The rest were missing or refused.
  - **V**: npc ids from the VMaNGOS world database
- **Status:** one guide, checked against the wiki where it could be read. Method has no guide for it yet.

## Notes and disagreements

- The two sides name their instance `stratholme`, and the side is the one whose boss is fought.
- Dathrohan's turn at 40%, the stun near him, and the undead after his death are K's.
- **Left out:** Skul, Hearthsinger Forresten and Stonespine (rares), Crimson Hammersmith and Black Guard Swordsmith (called by touching a pattern), Jarien and Sothos (Tier 0.5 quest). K lists Malor the Zealous where W has the Hammersmith; neither is written.
