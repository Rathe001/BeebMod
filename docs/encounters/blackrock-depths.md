## Notes and disagreements

- The order follows W's walk through the city. Runs differ a lot here. A fight starting shows its boss.
- **Ring of Law.** W says two waves, K three. The line gives no count. The six random bosses and Theldren are one entry.
- **Lord Roccor.** W says Ground Tremor silences for 2 seconds, K that it stuns. The line says it stops players and cuts off casts.
- **Hurley.** W kills Hurley first, K his helpers. Written as a disagreement.
- **Doomgrip.** W says four constructs in one place and three in another. The line gives no count.
- **Magmus.** W stacks behind him. K stands between the statues. The line says stay out of the fire rows.
- Moira is part of the Emperor's entry. She is immune to Polymorph (K).
- **Left out:** Panzor the Invincible (rare), Coren Direbrew (Brewfest).
