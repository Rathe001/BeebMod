# Ruins of Lordaeron

- **Key:** `ruinsoflordaeron` · new in Forever · 6 bosses (Viktor optional)
- **Sources:** W, M, K. The npc ids are K's (Forever pages).
- **Order:** Witherfang → The Baron → Viktor the Vile (fireplace event) → The Abandoned (statue event) → Bjork → Rath'mael. Each side has its own path. The bosses are the same.
- **Left out:** Lordaeron Captain (rare, Horde only).
