## Notes and disagreements

- **Jammal'an and Ogom.** W kills Ogom first. K kills Jammal'an first, as he turns a player against the group. Written as a disagreement.
- **Shade of Eranikus.** W faces him away from the group. K tanks him side-on. No facing line is written for the tank.
- Deep Slumber's 8 seconds and War Stomp's 4 are K's.
- The six balcony trolls are one entry. Their kills send no fight message, so the notes move on with Jammal'an's fight.
- Atal'alarion, Spawn of Hakkar and the Avatar are optional.
