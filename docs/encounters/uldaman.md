## Notes and disagreements

- **Ironaya and the Stone Keeper's immunity to damage over time** is W's alone. K doesn't contradict it.
- **Obsidian Sentinel.** W says two shards, at 66% and 33%. K says it splinters every so often. The line gives no count.
- **Sand Storms.** W calls them a slow and silence. K says one Sand Storm is summoned for 30 seconds. W's line is kept.
- **Grimlok's helpers.** W lists a Geomancer, a Brawler and a basilisk. K lists the basilisk. W's line is kept.
- War Stomp's 4 seconds, the Geologists running when nearly dead, and Shrink being a curse are K's.
