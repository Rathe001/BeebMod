# City of Dalaran

- **Key:** `cityofdalaran` · new in Forever · 8 bosses (Lyn the Ignored needs the Tome of Dalaran)
- **Sources:** W, M, K. The npc ids are K's (Forever pages).
- **Order:** Atrexis the Grave Knight → Lyn the Ignored → Mana Devourer → Arcane Anomaly → Fel Ancient → Arcanic Enigma → Unstable Sentinel → Shade of the Archmage. This is W's order. M lists them differently. The city is open, so a run picks its own.

## Disagreements

- **A boss's name.** W writes "Unstable Sentry", M and K "Unstable Sentinel". Data.lua matches both.
- **The archmage at the citadel.** W writes Archmage Modera, M "Modela". Modera is the known character, so W's spelling is used.
